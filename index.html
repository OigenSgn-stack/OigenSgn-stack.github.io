Shorts Studio Analytics

Shorts Studio Analytics is a private, self-hosted tool used by one creator to analyse the performance of their own YouTube channels.

It runs only on the creator's own computer. With the channel owner's permission it reads YouTube Analytics and YouTube Data (read-only) through Google's official APIs and stores the results locally. It does not upload, edit or delete anything on YouTube, and it is not offered to other users.
