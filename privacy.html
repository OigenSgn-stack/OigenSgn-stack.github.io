Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

What the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google and grants permission, Shorts Studio Analytics requests read-only access through these scopes:

  • youtube.readonly — list of the channel's videos and their public details;
  • yt-analytics.readonly — YouTube Analytics and Reporting data of the channel (views, watch time, retention, traffic sources, impressions and click-through rate);
  • yt-analytics-monetary.readonly — estimated revenue reports of the channel, where available.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to show the channel owner reports about their own channels. It is stored only on the owner's own computer, in a local database. Access tokens are stored locally and are never shared.

The data is not sold, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising. Parts of the reports may be shown to an AI assistant chosen by the owner when the owner asks for an analysis.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also complies with the YouTube API Services Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops all data collection; the locally stored data is deleted by deleting the local database file. Stored API data is refreshed or deleted in line with the YouTube API Services Developer Policies.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact details on the YouTube channels that use this tool.
